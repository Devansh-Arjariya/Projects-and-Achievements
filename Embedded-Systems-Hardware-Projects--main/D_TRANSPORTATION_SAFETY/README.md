# 🚆 Sub-Division: Transportation Safety & Smart Mobility

> **Railway track obstacle detection, autonomous emergency braking triggers, and intelligent parking bay guidance.**

---

## 🎯 Overview

Transportation safety and urban vehicular congestion represent two of the most pressing infrastructure challenges globally. This sub-division houses systems engineered to eliminate human error and optimize traffic flow:
1. **Railway Track Collision Prevention (Railway Obstacle Avoidance):** Developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2025** (reaching the national finalist stage) and showcased at **Vigyan Mela 2025** (where it earned an industry internship offer), this project integrates multi-sensor track monitoring to detect debris, vehicles, and wildlife on railway lines.
2. **Intelligent Urban Parking Guidance (Smart Parking System):** Developed during an industry engineering internship, this IoT system provides real-time bay occupancy detection to eliminate the search time and traffic congestion associated with parking.
